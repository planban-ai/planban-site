# Planban v2.0.0

Planban v2.0.0 adds online boards: take a board online to share it, open it in any browser or on your phone, and connect ChatGPT and Claude to it. Boards on your computer stay exactly as they are, free and with no account.

## Does Planban still work without an account?

Yes. Planban still runs on your computer with no account, and your boards stay there unless you take one online. Nothing about a local board is sent anywhere. Taking a board online is a choice for each board, and you can bring it back to your computer at any time with its full history.

## What are online boards?

- **Take a board online** from Board settings, **Take online**, or ask your agent. The whole board moves to your Planban account, Specs, Plans and history included, and your computer keeps a backup copy. Planban stays an index, not file storage: a board goes online once it holds only text, and Planban lists anything to tidy first.
- **Invite people** as editors or viewers, by email or with a link. Editors can change cards, Specs and Plans, restore from history and mark work Complete. Viewers can read everything and change nothing. Only the owner invites people, changes roles and takes the board online or back.
- **Each person chooses what their agents may do**: read, plan, or plan and complete. An agent can never do more than its person.
- **History follows the board.** Every change records who made it, and whether it was them or their agent.

See [Online boards](/docs/online-boards/).

## Can I use Planban in a browser or on my phone?

- Every online board has its own address at app.planban.ai, where you and the people you invite can read and edit it in any browser, with nothing to install. Log in with GitHub or an email code. New accounts start with a short checklist.
- **Your boards** lists your boards and the ones shared with you, with how many cards need you. Invitations waiting for you appear at the top, and you can start a new, empty board there.
- **On a phone** the board is one scrolling list grouped by column. A card opens full screen, Status and Move replace dragging, Save stays above the keyboard, and dialogs open as sheets.
- **Changes appear live.** When someone else or an agent changes the board, you see it without reloading.
- **If someone saves a Spec or Plan while you are editing it,** your text is kept and Planban shows you their version before you save again.
- Online boards in the Planban app on your computer have an **Open on the web** link in the board menu and Board settings.
- Dropdowns across the board and the online screens have been redesigned: as wide as their value, with the arrow beside the text, and the phone's own picker on a phone.

## Which agents and AI apps work with online boards?

- **Codex and Claude Code** with the Planban plugin work with online boards once your computer is signed in, through the same tools as local boards.
- **ChatGPT and claude.ai** connect to `https://app.planban.ai/mcp` as a custom connector: sign in and click Allow. Their search results link straight to the card on the web page.
- **Sign in on any device.** A computer or agent without a browser of its own, such as a remote, cloud or headless machine, can run `planban online login https://app.planban.ai --no-browser`. Planban shows a short code and a link. Open the link on any device where you are signed in, your phone included, check the code and tap Allow.
- **Access keys** for agents that cannot sign in themselves are created on the web page, in Connections. Each key reaches one board, says what it may do and when it expires, and is shown once.
- **Connections**, on the web page and in the app, lists every computer, browser, app and key signed in to your account, with when each was last used, and lets you cut any of them off, or all of them at once.

## What about privacy, security and price?

- A browser stays signed in for at most 7 days. Signing out, cutting a browser off in Connections, or **Cut off everything** ends it at once, and it needs a fresh sign-in.
- The web page loads only Planban's own files, its sign-in provider (Clerk) with its sign-in protection, and its live-update service. Its security policy refuses anything else. Invitation links are taken out of the address as soon as the page opens.
- Online boards are free at launch. Usage limits protect the service: an online board holds up to 256 MB, and each account can make up to 5,000 changes and write up to 64 MB of text a day.

See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for what the online service stores.

## How do I update to v2.0.0?

Refresh the marketplace and reinstall the plugin so the cached skills, hook scripts and MCP instructions update, then start a new session. This release adds dependencies, so updating from v1.1.7 runs a clean dependency installation. Existing local boards keep their storage format, so no migration is needed, and nothing goes online unless you take it there. See [Update Planban](/docs/updating/) for the commands in each host.

Projects whose `AGENTS.md` has a Planban block get its wording updated the next time a board moves online or back to your computer.
